What Salix does

Everything a modern storefront needs, built in.

From the first message to the final sale, Salix keeps buyers and sellers connected without ever leaving the app.

💬

Real-time chat

Buyers message sellers directly — text, photos, video, voice notes, even live voice and video calls, all built in.

📖

Stories & posts

Sellers share daily stories and posts to showcase new arrivals, the same way shoppers already browse social feeds.

🛍️

Unified storefronts

Every seller gets a clean, branded profile — products, reviews, followers, and conversations in one place.

🤖

Smart store assistant

An always-on assistant answers delivery, pricing, and policy questions instantly, trained on each seller's own rules.

📣

Built-in promotion

Sellers boost products, banners, and story ads to reach more shoppers — currently free for everyone on the platform.

🔒

Safe by design

Reporting, blocking, and admin review tools keep every conversation and transaction on Salix trustworthy.
